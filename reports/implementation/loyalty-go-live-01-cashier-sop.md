# Nafisah Rewards — Cashier SOP (V1)

**Document ID:** WN-LOYALTY-GO-LIVE-01-CASHIER-SOP  
**Date:** 2026-09-09  
**Audience:** Kasir

## Member sale

1. Ask customer phone (quietly)
2. Lookup / register if new
3. Attach member to order
4. Continue items → pay as usual
5. Points earn **automatically after payment** (when program active)

## Nonmember sale

1. Choose **Lewati** / tanpa member
2. Pay normally — same as before loyalty

Never force membership. Never use another customer’s number.

## Earn

- Automatic after successful payment when member attached and program on
- Small spend may earn **0** points (below Rp5.000 eligible) — still OK

## Redeem reward

1. Member must be attached
2. Choose eligible reward shown by system
3. Confirm reward on order (customer price Rp0)
4. Pay — system deducts points then may still earn points from paid merchandise
5. If reward unavailable / sold out: choose another or save points
6. If insufficient points: **do not override** — explain remaining points needed

## If loyalty error

- Complete sale with **Lewati** if possible so restaurant sales continue
- Do **not** promise manual points
- Escalate to owner with order time + phone masked

## Receipt

- May show masked member + points summary
- If QR present: customer can scan to check points (portal)
- Reprint does not give extra points

## Privacy

- Do not read full phone number loudly
- Do not share member points with other customers
- Loyalty signup ≠ permission for WhatsApp marketing
